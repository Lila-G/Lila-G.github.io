I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Bergen (Faculty of Medicine, Department of Clinical Science). My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, wearable devices, and digital health.

I currently lead research on continuous stress and endocrine monitoring from wearable signals, with a focus on modeling latent cortisol dynamics from multimodal physiological data.

## Current Position

Postdoctoral Researcher  
University of Bergen, Norway  
December 2024 to present

## Research Focus

- Machine learning for multimodal wearable sensor data
- Digital biomarkers for stress, endocrine, and metabolic health
- State-space and signal processing approaches for physiological modeling
- Translational digital medicine bridging engineering and clinical practice
